How to Cook Awesome Pizza

Nothing beats a homemade pizza fresh out of the oven. With a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and your favorite toppings, this recipe is simple enough for beginners while delivering restaurant-quality results.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1/2 cup tomato pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella chese
  • 20 slices pepperoni
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Fresh basil leaves (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a lightly floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza pan or baking stone.
  4. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Arrange the pepperoni on top.
  7. Season with oregano and garlic powder.
  8. Bake for 12–15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  9. Drizzle with olive oil and garnish with fresh basil before serving.

Serve the pizza while it's hot and enjoy it with a fresh salad or your favorite drink. Homemade pizza is perfect for family dinners, game nights, or whenever you're craving a classic comfort food.
