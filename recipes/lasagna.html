How to Make Delicious Lasagna

Lasagna is a timeless Italian comfort food made with layers of pasta, savory meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella cheese. This hearty dish is ideal for family gatherings or meal prep throughout the week.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 ounces marinara sauce
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Brown the ground beef with the onion and garlic in a skillet.
  4. Stir in the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  7. Add a layer of noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella.
  8. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  9. Sprinkle the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan on top.
  10. Bake for 40–45 minutes.
  11. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.

Lasagna tastes even better the next day, making it an excellent choice for leftovers. Pair it with garlic bread and a green salad for a complete meal.
