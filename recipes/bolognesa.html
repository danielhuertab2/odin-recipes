Cooking Fantastic Bolognesa

Spaghetti Bolognese is a hearty pasta dish featuring tender spaghetti coated in a rich, slow-simmered meat sauce. It's a comforting recipe that's easy to prepare and perfect for both weeknight dinners and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces spaghetti
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 24 ounces crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Cook the onion until softened, then add the garlic.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
  6. Simmer the sauce for 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Toss the cooked spaghetti with the sauce or spoon the sauce over the pasta.
  8. Garnish with grated Parmesan cheese and serve immediately.

Spaghetti Bolognese is a simple yet satisfying meal that's sure to become a family favorite. Enjoy it with warm garlic bread and freshly grated Parmesan for the best experience.
